Celys Care Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Our Commitment to Your Sanctuary

At Celys Care, your personal reflections, mood check-ins, journal entries, and conversations are sacred. We treat your personal mental wellness journey with the highest standards of confidentiality and security.

2. Data Encryption and Privacy

All sensitive user data—including journal reflections and check-in history—is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption and stored securely with database encryption at rest.

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your emotional data with advertisers. Your mental health metrics belong exclusively to you.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at support@celyscare.com.
